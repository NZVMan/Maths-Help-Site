Year 9 · Algebra

Expanding single brackets

What you'll learn

  • I can explain why expanding works, using arithmetic and the area of a rectangle.
  • I can expand a bracket with a positive number outside, such as \(3(x + 4)\).
  • I can expand a bracket with a negative number outside, such as \(-2(x - 5)\).
  • I can expand a bracket with a letter outside, such as \(x(x + 3)\) or \(2a(3a - b)\).
  • I can expand and simplify expressions with more than one bracket.
  • I can use expanding to solve problems.

Key words

Bracket
A pair of symbols \(( \ )\) that groups terms together. Everything inside is treated as one quantity.
Expand
Multiply out a bracket so the expression has no brackets.
Term
One part of an expression, together with the sign in front of it.
Like terms
Terms with exactly the same letters and powers.
Expand and simplify
Multiply out the brackets, then collect like terms.
Distributive law
The rule \(a(b + c) = ab + ac\): the term outside is shared out to every term inside.

1. Why expanding works

You already use expanding in mental arithmetic. To work out \(4 \times 23\), you can split 23 into \(20 + 3\):

\[ 4 \times 23 = 4 \times (20 + 3) = 4 \times 20 + 4 \times 3 = 80 + 12 = 92 \]

Algebra works the same way. \(4(x + 3)\) means 4 lots of \((x + 3)\). Picture it as the area of a rectangle 4 tall and \(x + 3\) wide.

Distributive law \[ a(b + c) = ab + ac \]

The term outside the bracket multiplies every term inside.

2. A positive number outside

Expanding

Expand (a) \(5(x + 2)\)   (b) \(3(2a - 7)\)   (c) \(4(3 + 2y)\).

Show solution
  1. \(5 \times x = 5x\) and \(5 \times 2 = 10\): \(5x + 10\)
  2. \(3 \times 2a = 6a\) and \(3 \times (-7) = -21\): \(6a - 21\)
  3. \(4 \times 3 = 12\) and \(4 \times 2y = 8y\): \(12 + 8y\)

Three terms inside

Expand \(6(x + y - 2)\).

Show solution
  1. Multiply each of the three terms by 6: \(6 \times x\), \(6 \times y\), \(6 \times (-2)\).

\(6x + 6y - 12\)

Quick check
  1. \(2(x + 9)\)
  2. \(7(3y - 1)\)
  3. \(5(a + 2b - 4)\)
Show answers
  1. \(2x + 18\)
  2. \(21y - 7\)
  3. \(5a + 10b - 20\)

3. A negative number outside

When the number outside is negative, it changes the sign of every term inside. Use the sign rules: a negative times a positive is negative, and a negative times a negative is positive.

Negative outside

Expand (a) \(-3(x + 4)\)   (b) \(-2(y - 5)\).

Show solution
  1. \(-3 \times x = -3x\) and \(-3 \times 4 = -12\): \(-3x - 12\)
  2. \(-2 \times y = -2y\) and \(-2 \times (-5) = +10\): \(-2y + 10\)

Just a minus sign

Expand \(-(x - 6)\).

Show solution
  1. A minus sign on its own in front of a bracket means \(-1\): \(-1(x - 6)\).
  2. \(-1 \times x = -x\) and \(-1 \times (-6) = +6\).

\(-x + 6\), or \(6 - x\)

Quick check
  1. \(-4(x + 1)\)
  2. \(-5(2y - 3)\)
  3. \(-(a + 8)\)
Show answers
  1. \(-4x - 4\)
  2. \(-10y + 15\)
  3. \(-a - 8\)

4. A letter outside

The term outside can be a letter, or a number and a letter. Multiply it by each term inside, using the rules for multiplying terms: \(x \times x = x^2\).

A letter outside

Expand (a) \(a(3 - a)\)   (b) \(2p(4p + 3)\)   (c) \(-y(y - 2)\).

Show solution
  1. \(a \times 3 = 3a\) and \(a \times (-a) = -a^2\): \(3a - a^2\)
  2. \(2p \times 4p = 8p^2\) and \(2p \times 3 = 6p\): \(8p^2 + 6p\)
  3. \(-y \times y = -y^2\) and \(-y \times (-2) = +2y\): \(-y^2 + 2y\)

Two different letters

Expand \(3x(2x - y + 4)\).

Show solution
  1. \(3x \times 2x = 6x^2\)
  2. \(3x \times (-y) = -3xy\)
  3. \(3x \times 4 = 12x\)

\(6x^2 - 3xy + 12x\)

Quick check
  1. \(x(x + 7)\)
  2. \(4m(m - 2)\)
  3. \(-a(a + b)\)
Show answers
  1. \(x^2 + 7x\)
  2. \(4m^2 - 8m\)
  3. \(-a^2 - ab\)

5. Expanding and simplifying

When an expression has more than one bracket, expand each one, then collect like terms. Be especially careful when a bracket has a minus sign in front of it.

Adding two brackets

Expand and simplify \(3(x + 2) + 4(x - 1)\).

Show solution
  1. Expand each bracket: \(3x + 6 + 4x - 4\).
  2. Collect like terms: \(3x + 4x = 7x\) and \(6 - 4 = 2\).

\(7x + 2\)

Subtracting a bracket

Expand and simplify \(5(a + 3) - 2(a - 4)\).

Show solution
  1. The second bracket is multiplied by \(-2\), not \(2\).
  2. \(5(a + 3) = 5a + 15\) and \(-2(a - 4) = -2a + 8\).
  3. Collect: \(5a - 2a = 3a\) and \(15 + 8 = 23\).

\(3a + 23\)

Squares and constants

Expand and simplify \(x(x + 2) + 3(x - 1)\).

Show solution
  1. Expand: \(x^2 + 2x + 3x - 3\).
  2. Collect: \(2x + 3x = 5x\).

\(x^2 + 5x - 3\)

Multiply before you add

Expand and simplify \(4 + 2(x - 3)\).

Show solution
  1. Order of operations: multiplication comes before addition, so expand the bracket first. Do not add \(4 + 2\).
  2. \(4 + 2x - 6\).
  3. Collect: \(4 - 6 = -2\).

\(2x - 2\)

Quick check
  1. \(2(x + 5) + 3(x + 1)\)
  2. \(6(y + 2) - 4(y + 1)\)
  3. \(3(2m - 1) - (m - 5)\)
  4. \(10 - 3(x + 2)\)
Show answers
  1. \(5x + 13\)
  2. \(2y + 8\)
  3. \(5m + 2\)
  4. \(4 - 3x\)

6. Using expanding

Mental arithmetic

Work out \(6 \times 98\) in your head.

Show solution
  1. \(98 = 100 - 2\), so \(6 \times 98 = 6(100 - 2) = 600 - 12\).

\(588\)

Expanding to solve an equation

Solve \(3(x + 2) = 21\).

Show solution
  1. Expand: \(3x + 6 = 21\).
  2. Subtract 6 from both sides: \(3x = 15\).
  3. Divide both sides by 3: \(x = 5\).
  4. Check: \(3(5 + 2) = 3 \times 7 = 21\). ✓

\(x = 5\)

You will learn more about this in Linear equations.

Exercise C Challenge

  1. Expand and simplify \(2x(3x - 4) - 3x(x - 5)\).
  2. Find the numbers \(a\) and \(b\) if \(a(2x + b) = 6x - 15\).
  3. Expand and simplify (a) \(\tfrac{1}{2}(4x - 6)\)   (b) \(\tfrac{1}{3}(9y + 12) - \tfrac{1}{2}(4y - 2)\).
  4. Prove that the sum of any five whole numbers in a row is 5 times the middle number. (Hint: call the middle number \(n\).)
  5. Expand and simplify \(x^2(x + 3) - x(x^2 - 2x)\).
  6. Find whole numbers to go in the boxes so that \(3(x + \square) - \square(x - 1)\) simplifies to \(x + 11\).
Show answers

  2. \(2a = 6\), so \(a = 3\); \(3b = -15\), so \(b = -5\)
  3. (a) \(2x - 3\) (b) \(3y + 4 - 2y + 1 = y + 5\)

  5. \(x^3 + 3x^2 - x^3 + 2x^2 = 5x^2\)
  6. \(3(x + 3) - 2(x - 1) = 3x + 9 - 2x + 2 = x + 11\), so the boxes are 3 and 2.

Common mistakes

Only multiplying the first term

\(4(x + 3)\) is \(4x + 12\), not \(4x + 3\). The number outside multiplies every term inside.

Signs with a negative outside

\(-2(x - 5) = -2x + 10\). A negative times a negative is positive.

Subtracting a bracket

In \(5(a + 3) - 2(a - 4)\), the second bracket is multiplied by \(-2\), so the last term is \(+8\), not \(-8\).

Adding before multiplying

\(5 - 2(x + 1)\) is not \(3(x + 1)\). Expand first: \(5 - 2x - 2 = 3 - 2x\).

Summary

  • Expanding means multiplying out brackets: \(a(b + c) = ab + ac\).
  • The term outside multiplies every term inside, including its sign.
  • A negative outside changes the sign of every term inside.
  • A letter outside can give squared terms: \(x(x + 5) = x^2 + 5x\).
  • "Expand and simplify" means expand every bracket, then collect like terms.
  • Multiply out brackets before adding or subtracting.
